# SeatSniper voice-alert audio

- `seat-alert.ogg`: original phone-style double-ring followed by a locally
  synthesized macOS Samantha voice: “Seat Sniper alert. There is an update for
  your seats. Please open Discord.” No external speech service was used.
- `seat-ringtone.ogg`: original 440/480 Hz double-ring, generated by
  `scripts/make-seat-ringtone.py`. No recorded ringtone or third-party sample.
- 48 kHz stereo Opus. Full clip: 8.93 seconds, measured -18.06 LUFS integrated,
  -6.19 dBTP after encoding. A single linear gain was used to reduce loudness.
- The full message plays once per new alert; subsequent repeats use only the
  ringtone. Replies in the bot DM or owner interaction acknowledgements stop it.
- Designed using the procedural-sfx skill's attack/envelope and measurement
  guidance. Short fades prevent clicks. Live listening is required to accept
  the final voice/ringtone; numeric loudness checks alone do not judge quality.
